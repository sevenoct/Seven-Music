## What's new

- Updates: Seven now looks for new versions by itself, downloads them, and shows what changed. Settings has a new **Updates** section with a **Check now** button.
- The Home carousel's button now says **Pause** while the song it shows is playing, and **Play** when it isn't.
- Settings: the title and the search box stay at the top while the page scrolls.
- The "no account" picture and Settings' Account group use the same icon, and Settings' Discord icon is now Discord's own mark.
